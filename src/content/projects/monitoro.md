### TL;DR / Quick Answer

> **What is Monitoro?**  
> Monitoro is a lightweight, high-availability website and API monitoring service that pings endpoints at set intervals and sends instant outage alerts directly to your Telegram chat or team channel the second an incident occurs.
>
> - **Who is it for?** Indie hackers, developers, system administrators, and small businesses who need 24/7 uptime monitoring without expensive enterprise SaaS fees.
> - **How does it notify you?** Via Telegram Bot API webhooks, eliminating delays associated with spammy email alerts.
> - **Platform:** Web dashboard + Telegram Bot ([www.monitoro.online](https://www.monitoro.online)).

---

### Key Questions & Answers (AEO)

#### Why choose Telegram alerts over email alerts?
Email notifications often get delayed, buried under newsletter spam, or delayed by inbox filtering. Telegram push notifications arrive in sub-seconds with audible alerts, enabling immediate incident resolution.

#### What types of checks does Monitoro perform?
- **HTTP / HTTPS Response Checks:** Validates status codes (200 OK, 301/302 redirects, 4xx, 5xx server errors).
- **SSL Certificate Expiry:** Alerts you before critical SSL certificates expire.
- **Latency & Response Timing:** Tracks degradation in page response time.
