### Overview

**FlowRupee** is an all-in-one personal finance app designed specifically for everyday Indian consumers, roommates, freelancers, and small teams. It eliminates the hassle of tracking where your money goes by providing instant expense logging, intelligent categorization, split bill management, and visual budget analytics.

Whether you are tracking daily chai and grocery expenses or managing shared flat rent, electricity, and trips with friends, FlowRupee keeps your finances clear, balanced, and completely under your control.

---

### Key Features & Highlights

1. **Lightning-Fast Expense & Income Logging:**
   - Log any transaction in under 3 seconds with custom categories (Food, Travel, Bills, Shopping, Investments, Rent).
   - Support for custom payment methods: UPI, Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, NetBanking.

2. **Group Split & Roommate Settlements:**
   - Split shared expenses with roommates or friends effortlessly.
   - Calculates who owes whom with minimal transactions so you never have awkward payment reminders.

3. **Visual Analytics & Spending Insights:**
   - Interactive charts breakdown your monthly spending patterns.
   - Identify money leaks and track how close you are to your budget limits.

4. **100% Privacy & Security:**
   - Your financial data is private and secure.
   - Biometric screen lock (Fingerprint / Face Unlock) to keep your records safe from prying eyes.

5. **Offline-First Resilience:**
   - Works seamlessly offline without an active internet connection. Automatically syncs when you're back online.
