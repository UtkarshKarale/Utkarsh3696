### TL;DR / Quick Answer

> **What is LeadPluck?**  
> LeadPluck is a local-first Chrome extension that automatically extracts publicly listed contact details (phone numbers, emails, addresses, ratings, and websites) from Google Maps listings and public web pages into formatted CSV lists.
>
> - **Who is it for?** Agencies, B2B sales professionals, freelance service providers, and local market researchers.
> - **How does it protect privacy?** All search indexing and contact parsing executes directly inside your browser session. No scraped data or user browsing behavior is sent to third-party cloud servers.
> - **Platform:** Browser Extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave) with landing page at [/leadpluck](/leadpluck).

### Key Questions & Answers (AEO)

#### Does LeadPluck bypass paywalls or private data?
No. LeadPluck strictly extracts publicly posted contact data that businesses intentionally publish on their public business profiles and public website contact pages.

#### How does LeadPluck prevent duplicate contacts?
LeadPluck features an automated duplicate detection algorithm that matches business names, phone numbers, and domains to provide a merge preview before CSV export.

### Project Description

LeadPluck is a high-speed, privacy-focused browser extension designed to help creators, freelancers, agencies, and businesses build clean lists of local business contacts without tedious manual copy-pasting.

Public business directories and map listings contain valuable contact details. LeadPluck extracts these details directly from your browser session, stores everything securely on your own device, and allows you to filter and export them to structured CSV files.

### Key Highlights

- **Google Maps Capture:** Capture business name, address, phone number, website, rating, and review counts with one click.
- **Automated Batch Search Runner:** Queue multiple search terms across multiple cities (e.g. "IT companies in Pune", "Cooperative credit societies in Kolhapur"). LeadPluck runs them sequentially.
- **Deep Website Scanner:** Crawls public business websites to discover email addresses, phone numbers, WhatsApp, and social media links on Contact and About pages.
- **Custom Lists & Segmented CSV:** Group leads into custom lists and export them to clean CSV spreadsheets.
- **Intelligent Duplicate Finder:** Identifies repeat businesses with an interactive merge preview.
- **100% Local-First Privacy:** All data is saved inside your browser's local extension storage. Zero remote databases, zero analytics, zero external servers.
